## Current Safeguards

All public source data remains untrusted until independently verified. The workflow never fabricates eligibility, experience, media, language ability, or contact information. It never sends emails, DMs, WhatsApp messages, applications, payments, posts, uploads, or links externally. The selected Instagram Reel may be used only as an optional portfolio link where a verified brief explicitly permits it, and never as a self-tape substitute.
