# Google AI Automation Brief

Use this as a standing instruction in a Google AI workspace only after its account access and scheduling surfaces are available.

> Review up to ten legitimate public casting offices, casting-company accounts, or active role-specific briefs per run, with a daily maximum of twenty sources. Prioritize official sources, clear eligibility, deadlines, published application routes, and scam-risk checks. Record source, role, requirements, deadline, route, and factual risk notes in the tracker. Prepare a role-specific draft or self-tape plan only when the source is official, the route is clear, and every identity, availability, language, skill, contact, and media requirement is verified. Never invent facts. Never send DMs, emails, WhatsApp messages, form submissions, payments, documents, or social posts; never bypass closed forms, pay fees, use mass outreach, or publish content automatically. Report concise actionable findings and material gaps for manual review.

## Scheduling Guidance

Use a low-frequency schedule that runs no more than twice daily. Keep results in the workspace for review and synchronize only factual tracker updates. Do not use high-frequency polling or schedules that post, message, submit, or pay automatically.
