# Gemini Spark Internal Readiness Prompt

Use this only in an eligible personal Gemini Spark account after confirming the account’s schedule feature is available.

> Every day, review only the actor-profile materials that are already present in my connected internal Drive or Workspace sources. Create an internal readiness report with: (1) missing profile facts such as playing age, city, languages, height, availability, and contact details; (2) missing or expired material such as headshots, introduction video, role-specific self-tapes, and skill demonstrations; (3) a prioritized preparation checklist; and (4) any public casting opportunity listed in the internal tracker that cannot progress because required factual material is missing. Do not search for new casting calls, send messages, create applications, upload or share files, publish media, pay fees, or claim anything not present in the source material. Return the report inside Gemini only.

## Suggested Schedule

Run once daily at 13:00 Asia/Kolkata, between the existing 09:00 and 18:00 casting-research windows.
