# Profile Kit Checklist & Shooting Tips

This checklist helps ensure your acting profile kit is complete and professional. A strong profile kit is crucial for making a great first impression.

## 📸 Headshots

*   **Variety:** Include at least 3-5 different looks (commercial, theatrical, character).
*   **Quality:** High-resolution, professionally taken photos.
*   **Expression:** Showcase a range of emotions and personality.
*   **Current:** Headshots should accurately reflect your current appearance.
*   **Styling:** Wardrobe and makeup should be appropriate for the character types you portray.
*   **Retouching:** Minimal and natural-looking retouching.

### Shooting Tips for Headshots:
*   **Lighting:** Soft, even lighting is key. Avoid harsh shadows.
*   **Background:** Simple, uncluttered backgrounds that don't distract from your face.
*   **Eye Contact:** Engage with the camera. Your eyes are the window to your soul!
*   **Comfort:** Relax and let your personality shine through. A comfortable actor takes better photos.

## 🎬 Acting Reel

*   **Length:** Keep it concise, ideally 1-3 minutes. Casting directors have limited time.
*   **Best Work First:** Start with your strongest scene to immediately grab attention.
*   **Variety of Roles:** Showcase your range with different characters and genres.
*   **High Quality:** Ensure good audio and video quality. Poor quality can be distracting.
*   **Clear Identification:** Include a title card with your name and contact info at the beginning and end.
*   **Recent Work:** Prioritize your most recent and relevant performances.

### Shooting Tips for Reels:
*   **Sound:** Clear, audible dialogue is paramount. Use external microphones if possible.
*   **Editing:** Professional editing makes a huge difference. Seamless transitions and good pacing.
*   **Storytelling:** Even short clips should convey a sense of story or character arc.
*   **Self-Tapes:** If using self-tapes, ensure good lighting, clean background, and clear audio.

## 🗣️ Monologues

*   **Selection:** Choose material that suits your type and showcases your strengths. Avoid overdone pieces.
*   **Length:** Typically 1-2 minutes. Keep it engaging.
*   **Contrast:** If submitting multiple, aim for contrasting pieces (e.g., comedic and dramatic).
*   **Preparation:** Fully memorized and deeply understood. Show character depth.

### Performance Tips for Monologues:
*   **Objective:** Know what your character wants and why they want it.
*   **Relationship:** Who are you talking to? What is your relationship with them?
*   **Environment:** Where are you? What are the circumstances?
*   **Pacing:** Vary your pace and intensity to keep the audience engaged.

## 🎥 Introduction Video (Self-Tape Intro)

*   **Purpose:** A brief, engaging video to introduce yourself and your personality.
*   **Length:** 30-60 seconds is ideal.
*   **Content:** State your name, where you're based, and a brief, authentic statement about yourself or your acting.
*   **Authenticity:** Let your natural personality come through. Don't try to be someone you're not.

### Shooting Tips for Introduction Videos:
*   **Lighting:** Face a window or use soft, even lighting.
*   **Background:** Clean, neutral background. Avoid distractions.
*   **Audio:** Clear audio is essential. Speak clearly and at a good volume.
*   **Framing:** Mid-chest to just above your head. Look directly into the camera.
*   **Energy:** Be energetic and positive, but natural. Smile!

--- 

**Remember:** Your profile kit is your calling card. Invest time and effort into making it as strong as possible!
