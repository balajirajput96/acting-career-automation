## Video direction

Editorial office-science look: navy base, warm-cream type, teal for an intentional work goal, coral for interruption signals, and yellow for an optional work-design step. Captions remain in a dark high-contrast lower band. Motion uses short card handoffs and measured pauses, never flashing alert effects.
