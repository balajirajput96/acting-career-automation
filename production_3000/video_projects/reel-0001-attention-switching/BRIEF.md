# Reel_0001 — Study ke beech baar-baar switch karne ka cost

**Format:** 60-second vertical 1080×1920 faceless Hindi explainer.

**Audience:** Hindi-speaking students and working learners.

**Core teaching point:** Experimental task-switching studies commonly find slower and/or less accurate performance after a task change than after repeating the same task. The Reel invites viewers to test a single-task focus block; it does not promise marks, diagnose attention conditions, or make permanence claims about attention.

**Evidence class:** Established experimental pattern with context-dependent interpretation. Source ledger: `../../source_ledgers/Reel_0001_attention_task_switching.md`.

**Identity boundary:** No user face or personal voice. Narration is neutral synthetic Hindi voice; all visuals are original HTML motion graphics.
