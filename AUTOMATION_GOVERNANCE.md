# Automation Governance

## Purpose

This repository supports a compliant acting-career workflow: public casting research, scam screening, lead tracking, issue-based draft preparation, and manual review. It is designed to improve organization and response readiness without automating communications to casting contacts.

## What Can Run Automatically

Scheduled workflows may review the local lead tracker, identify new rows, flag scam-risk signals, open review issues, create follow-up reminders, and update tracking labels. External research results may be saved as facts only when the source, role, route, deadline, and risk note are clear.

## What Must Remain Manual

No workflow may send Instagram DMs, email, WhatsApp messages, application forms, files, payments, or social-media posts. It may not place orders, bid for work, accept contracts, publish edited videos, bypass closed forms, pay any fee, or use a generic Reel as a role-specific self-tape. Every external submission requires a complete truthful package and a context-specific final safeguard.

## Source Quality Standard

Prefer public posts or pages owned by official casting offices, production teams, or established platforms. Record the published application route, deadline, role criteria, location, fee language, and any unanswered credibility question. Treat requests for money, missing project details, generic consumer-email routes, and mismatched identity requirements as risk signals.

## GitHub Review Model

Use one issue per verified lead. Include the factual source link, role, requirements, risk notes, allowed portfolio material, and missing assets. Status labels may track `new`, `researching`, `ready-for-review`, `sent-tracking`, `callback`, or `rejected`; issues are not automatically closed.
