# Video Editing Workflow

## Asset Classification

Every source clip must be labelled before use as either: a general portfolio Reel, an introduction video, a self-tape, a role-specific audition, or an edited social cut. A clip may not be relabelled to satisfy a brief it was not recorded for.

## Editing Brief

For a general acting portfolio cut, prioritize clear audio, well-lit close-ups, stable framing, and uninterrupted performance. Preserve original dialogue; do not add claims, credits, character names, or captions that cannot be verified. Keep export versions separate from raw footage and log the source files, edit date, and intended permitted use.

## Role-Specific Self-Tape Rule

When a casting brief requests a scene, mid-shot, specified duration, language, introduction, skill demonstration, or character improvisation, record a new clip that follows those instructions. Existing Instagram Reels may only be linked where the brief expressly permits a general Instagram or portfolio link.

## Publishing Boundary

Edited videos can be queued for review with title, caption, platform, and intended audience. They must not be published to Instagram, Facebook, or another platform automatically. Publishing and paid boosts are external actions that require an interaction-specific safeguard.
