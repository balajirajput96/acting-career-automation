# Script — What Stress Can Feel Like in the Body

**Status:** `INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY — NOT POSTED`

## Narration

Jab hum stress mein hote hain, toh yeh sirf dimag tak seemit nahi rehta. Hamari body bhi physical signals ke zariye react karti hai. Common signs mein shamil hain muscle tension, jaise neck aur shoulders mein stiffness, headaches, aur rapid breathing. Kuch logon ko stomach discomfort ya fatigue bhi mehsoos hoti hai. Yeh physical reactions natural hain, kyunki body ka nervous system fight-or-flight response activate karta hai. Jab stress lambe samay tak bane rahe, toh yeh health par asar dal sakta hai. Apne body ke signals ko pehchanna aur samajhna mental wellness ka pehla step hai. Agar physical symptoms lagataar bane rahein, toh kisi qualified healthcare professional se consult karein.
