## Video Direction

Use an editorial learning-lab style: midnight navy ground, paper-cream text, citron learning markers, and coral caution markers. Motion is restrained and diagrammatic: cards slide into a timeline, traces reappear through opacity and scale, and each caption is placed above the lower safe area. No faces, stock imagery, medical imagery, brain scans, user identity, or exaggerated “brain-hack” claims.
