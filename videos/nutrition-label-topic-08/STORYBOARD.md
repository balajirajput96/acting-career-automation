## Video direction

The look is an editorial grocery-notebook: midnight navy ground, warm paper cards, bright citrus highlights, and small coral warnings. Motion is restrained and explanatory—labels slide into place, gauges rise, and data rows reveal one at a time. There is no product branding, fear language, or health-outcome imagery.
