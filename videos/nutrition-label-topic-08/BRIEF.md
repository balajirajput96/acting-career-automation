# Topic 08 — Reading a packaged-food nutrition label

## Locked production brief

Create a 60-second, faceless, 9:16 Hindi-English educational explainer for a general audience. The visual system uses invented U.S.-style label diagrams, not a real product label or medical claim. The production describes serving size, Percent Daily Value, nutrient comparisons, and an ingredients list as general literacy tools.

## Safety boundaries

This is general education only. The video does not diagnose, prescribe a diet, promise health outcomes, or replace personalized advice. It explicitly states that labels vary by country and product, and it directs viewers with allergies or individual dietary concerns to qualified support.

## Format

- Canvas: 1080×1920 vertical.
- Duration: 60 seconds.
- Voice: neutral synthetic Hindi-English narration; no user face or personal voice.
- Visuals: CSS, SVG-like shapes, typography, and invented food-label graphics only.
