Fiber kya hai aur ye hamari health ke liye kyun zaroori hai? Dietary fiber ek plant-based nutrient hai jo body digest nahi karti, lekin digestion ko smooth rakhne mein vital role play karti hai. Simple food sources mein whole grains jaise oats, dalen, beans, aur fresh fruits shamil hain. Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health ke mutabik, fiber blood sugar levels ko stable rakhne aur cholesterol ko manage karne mein madad karta hai. NHS ke anusar, regular high-fiber diet heart health aur gut function ko support karti hai. Apni daily diet mein simple whole foods include karna ek effective step hai. Yeh educational awareness ke liye hai, clinical prescription nahi.
