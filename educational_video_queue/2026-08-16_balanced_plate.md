# Daily Educational Video Package — A Balanced Plate Is a Guide, Not a Prescription

**Status:** `READY FOR INTERNAL ASSET REVIEW — NOT PUBLISHED`

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 60 seconds, Hindi-English mix, faceless visual draft until an authorized high-quality face/voice source file is selected.

## Educational Purpose

Explain that a balanced plate is a flexible visual guide for general meal quality, not a calorie plan, medical diet, or one-size-fits-all prescription.

## Source Ledger

| Source | Claim used | Limitation carried into script |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WHO — Healthy diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet) | WHO describes adequacy, balance, moderation, and diversity as core healthy-diet principles and notes that healthy diets vary with individual and cultural context. | This is general population guidance; needs may differ with medical conditions, pregnancy, age, activity, allergies, and clinical care. |
| [Harvard T.H. Chan — Healthy Eating Plate](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/healthy-eating-plate/) | The plate is presented as a guide for balanced meals, with vegetables/fruits, whole grains, and protein as approximate visual components. | It is not a calorie prescription; individual needs vary. |
| [NHS — Eating a balanced diet](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/how-to-eat-a-balanced-diet/eating-a-balanced-diet/) | General guidance emphasizes variety across food groups and notes that people with special needs or a medical condition should seek professional advice. | This video does not create a personal diet plan or tell viewers what amount they should eat. |

## Narration Script

> **Hook:** “Perfect diet” chase karne se better hai ek simple question: plate mein variety hai ya nahi?
>
> WHO ke four ideas yaad rakho: adequacy, balance, moderation, aur diversity. Harvard ka Healthy Eating Plate ek visual guide deta hai—roughly, plate ka bada hissa vegetables aur fruits; baaki mein whole grains aur protein sources.
>
> Lekin yeh calorie chart ya strict rulebook nahi hai. Aapki age, activity, culture, budget, allergies, aur medical needs matter karti hain.
>
> Aaj ka simple step: apne next meal mein ek colorful vegetable ya fruit, ek fibre-rich staple, aur ek protein source add karne ka socho.
>
> Agar aapko medical condition, eating concern, ya personalized plan chahiye, doctor ya registered dietitian se baat karein. Sources caption mein hain.
