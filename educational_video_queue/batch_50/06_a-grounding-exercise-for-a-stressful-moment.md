## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
This educational video package is strictly for informational and educational purposes. It does not diagnose mental health conditions, prescribe psychiatric treatment, promise guaranteed results, claim clinical cures, or substitute for professional medical or psychological care. If you or someone you know is experiencing severe psychological distress, panic disorders, or safety concerns, please consult a licensed mental health professional or emergency medical services immediately.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (120 Words)
> Jab stress ya anxiety badhti hai, toh dimag current moment se door bhagne lagta hai. Aise stressful moment mein, ek simple grounding exercise aapko wapas present mein la sakti hai. Isse 5-4-3-2-1 technique kehte hain. 
> 
> Sabse pehle, apne aas-paas dekhiye aur paanch aisi cheezein note kijiye jo aap dekh sakte hain. 
> 
> Dusra, chaar aisi cheezein mehsoos kijiye jo aap apni skin par touch kar rahe hain—jaise chair ya hawa. 
> 
> Teesra, teen aisi aawazein suniye jo aapke aas-paas chal rahi hain. 
> 
> Chautha, do aisi cheezein mehsoos kijiye jinki khushboo aapko pata ho. 
> 
> Aur aakhiri, ek cheez ka taste note kijiye. 
> 
> Ye sensory exercise aapke nervous system ko calm karti hai. Yaad rakhiye, yeh professional mental health care ka substitute nahi hai.
