## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
> **Educational Notice**: This educational-video package is strictly for informational and mental-health literacy purposes. It does not diagnose medical or psychological conditions, prescribe treatments, promise specific outcomes, claim cures, or substitute for professional clinical care. For individuals experiencing acute distress or mental health crises, appropriate crisis support resources and professional help must be sought immediately.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (112 Words)
Jab aap stress ya mental pressure feel karte hain, toh kisi trusted adult ya professional se baat karna ek positive aur safe step hota hai. Mental health support ke liye, aapke school counselor, family doctor, ya kisi caring adult par bharosa karna aapko proper guidance deta hai. Apne feelings ko openly express karne mein hesitation normal hai, par clear sharing se situations easy ho jati hain. Professional guidance seek karna strength ki nishani hai, weakness ki nahi. Agar aapko immediate emotional support chahiye, toh official helpline par contact karein. Yeh educational overview professional medical diagnosis ya treatment ko replace nahi karta, balki positive well-being aur safe help-seeking habits ko encourage karta hai.
