## 4. Narration Script (Hindi-English)
*Word Count: 118 words*

Jab aap ek sath multiple tasks karte hain, toh lagta hai productivity badh rahi hai. Lekin neuroscience kehta hai ki human brain simultaneous multitasking ke liye design nahi hai. American Psychological Association ke anusar, jab hum ek task se dusre task par switch karte hain, toh brain ko har bar context shift karna padta hai. Isse switching cost kehte hain, jo productivity aur working memory ko reduce karta hai. Stanford University ki research batati hai ki constant task switching se focus weaken ho jaati hai. Brain actual mein multitasking nahi karta; yeh rapid task switching karta hai jo cognitive load badhata hai aur errors create karta hai. Isliye, deep work aur efficiency ke liye monotasking best approach hai.
