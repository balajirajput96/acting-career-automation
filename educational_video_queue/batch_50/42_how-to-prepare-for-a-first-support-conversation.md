- **Support & Safety Cue:** The content emphasizes boundary setting and explicitly advises connecting individuals with professional mental health resources or crisis helplines when immediate risk or critical distress is observed.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (122 Words)
Jab aap kisi aise vyakti se baat karna chahte hain jo mental health struggles se guzar raha hai, toh preparation zaroori hai. Sabse pehle, ek secure aur private jagah chunein jahan distraction kam ho. Apne intentions clear rakhein: aapka goal advice dena nahi, balki sunna aur support karna hai. Non-judgmental attitude ke sath active listening practice karein. Unki baat ko beech mein na kaatein, aur feelings ko validate karein. Yaad rakhein, aap professional therapist nahi hain; aapka kaam ek compassionate listener banna hai. Agar situation critical lage ya immediate risk ho, toh professional mental health support ya helpline ki madad lene ke liye encourage karein. Proper boundaries aur patience ke sath kiya gaya yeh pehla conversation ek safe anchor ban sakta hai.
