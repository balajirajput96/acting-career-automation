## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script (Word Count: 119)
> Galti hona human nature ka ek normal hissa hai. Jab hum mistake karte hain, toh hum aksar khud ke sabse harsh critic ban jaate hain. Lekin research yeh batati hai ki harsh self judgment anxiety aur stress badhata hai. Iske bajaye, jab aap apne sath self compassion rakhte hain, toh aap mistakes se behtar seekhte hain. Harvard Medical School aur UC Berkeley ki research ke mutabiq, iske teen pillars hain: khud ko judge karne ke bajaye kindness dena, yeh samajhna ki imperfection shared human experience hai, aur thoughts ko mindfulness ke sath accept karna. Agli baar jab koi galti ho, toh apne aap se wahi gentle tone use karein jo kisi close friend ke liye ho. Yeh resilience build karta hai. Agar persistent distress ho, toh professional support lein.
