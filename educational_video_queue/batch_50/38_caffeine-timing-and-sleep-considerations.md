## Limitations and Safety Considerations

This educational review package strictly adheres to clinical communication guidelines and public health standards. The material is intended exclusively for general educational literacy and awareness. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatments, promise specific health outcomes, claim cures, employ fear-based or sensational language, provide individualized nutritional or medical advice, or serve as a substitute for professional healthcare consultation. Furthermore, individual sensitivities, metabolic rates, and tolerances to caffeine vary substantially due to genetic differences, habitual intake levels, and underlying physiological factors.

## Hindi-English Script

The following script is written for neutral faceless narration in a balanced Hindi-English (bilingual) register, calibrated precisely to **124 words** to fit comfortably within a 60-second delivery window at a measured, professional speaking pace.

> Aaj hum baat karenge caffeine timing aur sleep considerations ke bare mein. Caffeine ek stimulant hai jo alertness badhata hai, par iska consumption neend ko kaise impact karta hai, yeh samajhna zaroori hai. Research kehti hai ki caffeine ka half-life five to six hours hota hai, aur yeh body mein kayi ghante active rehta hai. Sleep Foundation recommend karta hai ki bedtime se eight hours pehle caffeine avoid karein. Ek clinical study ke mutabiq, agar aap sleep time se six hours pehle bhi caffeine lete hain, toh yeh total sleep time aur sleep quality ko significantly reduce kar sakta hai. Isliye, afternoon ke baad coffee ya tea intake limit karna sleep hygiene ke liye important hai. Listen to your body and maintain healthy habits!
