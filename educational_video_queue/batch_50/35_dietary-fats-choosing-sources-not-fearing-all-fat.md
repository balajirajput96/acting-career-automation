## 3. Limitations and Safety Framework

> **Clinical and Educational Disclaimer:** This educational review package is designed strictly for informational and literacy-building purposes. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatments, promise specific health outcomes, claim cures, employ fear-based or sensational rhetoric, provide individualized clinical nutrition advice, or substitute for professional medical guidance. Viewers with pre-existing metabolic or cardiovascular conditions are advised to consult qualified healthcare professionals before altering their dietary habits.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (110 Words)

The script is written in a neutral, professional, bilingual (Hindi-English) narration style suited for faceless educational video production.

> "Dietary fats ko le kar ek common misconception yeh hai ki sabhi fats body ke liye harmful hote hain. Lekin nutritional science yeh kehti hai ki fat ek essential macronutrient hai. Humein fat se poori tarah darna nahi chahiye, balki sahi sources choose karne par dhyan dena chahiye. Healthy fats, jaise monounsaturated aur polyunsaturated fats, jo nuts, seeds, olive oil aur fatty fish mein milte hain, heart health aur overall cellular function ko support karte hain. Iske mukable, saturated fats ko limit karna aur industrial trans fats ko avoid karna behad zaroori hai. Asal mein, key yeh hai ki aap fat ki quality par focus karein aur smart selections banayein."
