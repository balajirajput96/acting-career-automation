## Source Ledger

1. **Caring for Your Mental Health**
   - **Organization:** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
   - **URL:** https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Regular physical activity, structured daily routines, and healthy sleep patterns directly support mood stabilization and overall mental wellbeing.

2. **5 steps to mental wellbeing**
   - **Organization:** NHS (National Health Service)
   - **URL:** https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/five-steps-to-mental-wellbeing/
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Incorporating small, actionable daily practices—such as physical activity, connecting with others, and paying attention to the present moment—improves mental health.

3. **How a daily routine can boost your mental health**
   - **Organization:** UCLA Health
   - **URL:** https://www.uclahealth.org/news/article/how-daily-routine-can-boost-your-mental-health
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Consistency in daily habits, including stable sleep schedules and morning routines, reduces daily stress and builds emotional resilience.

## Limitations & Safety Note
This video content is strictly educational and intended for general wellbeing awareness. It does not diagnose mental health conditions, prescribe medical treatment, claim cures, make individualized dietary or medical recommendations, or substitute for professional clinical care. 

*Support & Urgent Safety Cue:* If you or someone you know is experiencing persistent emotional distress, crisis, or mental health challenges, please reach out to a qualified healthcare professional or a trusted helpline (e.g., Tele-MANAS helpline in India at 14416).

## Hindi-English Script (Faceless Narration)
**Word Count:** 124 words

Kya aapko lagta hai ki ek achhi wellbeing routine banana mushkil hai? Let's keep it simple and realistic. Mental health experts suggest ki chhote steps daily life mein bada farak la sakte hain. Sabse pehle, sleep consistency par dhyan dein. Roz ek hi time par sona aur uthna body ke internal clock ko support karta hai. Second, apni daily routine mein 20 se 30 minutes ki physical activity jodiein—chahe wo simple walk ho ya stretching. Third, thoda time mindfulness ya present moment awareness ke liye nikalein. Yaad rakhein, wellbeing routine ka matlab perfection nahi, balki consistency hai. Agar aapko persistent emotional stress feel ho raha hai, toh professional support lene mein bilkul hichkichayein nahi. Tele-MANAS helpline 14416 par bhi sampark kar sakte hain. Take care of your mind every single day.
