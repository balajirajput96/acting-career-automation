## 2. Authoritative Source Ledger
1. **Source Title:** I'm So Stressed Out! Fact Sheet
   * **Organization:** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH), U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
   * **URL:** https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/so-stressed-out-fact-sheet
   * **Precise Supported Claim:** Stress functions as an internal reaction that frequently manifests through physical symptoms such as headaches, muscle tension, and sleep disturbances.

2. **Source Title:** Stress symptoms: Effects on your body and behavior
   * **Organization:** Mayo Clinic
   * **URL:** https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/stress-management/in-depth/stress-symptoms/art-20050987
   * **Precise Supported Claim:** The body's autonomic nervous system activation during stress commonly results in physical signs including muscle stiffness, headaches, fatigue, and gastrointestinal discomfort.

3. **Source Title:** Identifying and relieving stress
   * **Organization:** Harvard Health Publishing (Harvard Medical School)
   * **URL:** https://www.health.harvard.edu/topics/stress
   * **Precise Supported Claim:** Chronic or unmanaged stress regularly presents through recurring everyday physical issues such as chest tightness, stomachaches, and muscle pain.

## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script
* **Word Count:** 119 words (Target: 105–125 words)
* **Tone:** Neutral, calm, professional, and accessible faceless narration

> Jab hum stress mein hote hain, toh yeh sirf dimag tak seemit nahi rehta. Hamari body bhi physical signals ke zariye react karti hai. Common signs mein shamil hain muscle tension, jaise neck aur shoulders mein stiffness, headaches, aur rapid breathing. Kuch logon ko stomach discomfort ya fatigue bhi mehsoos hoti hai. Yeh physical reactions natural hain, kyunki body ka nervous system 'fight or flight' response activate karta hai. Jab stress lambe samay tak bane rahe, toh yeh health par asar dal sakta hai. Apne body ke signals ko pehchanna aur samajhna mental wellness ka pehla step hai. Agar physical symptoms lagataar bane rahein, toh kisi qualified healthcare professional se consult karein. Aap akele nahi hain; support available hai.
