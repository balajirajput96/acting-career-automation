## 4. Hindi-English Script (Neutral Faceless Narration)
*Word Count: 117 words*

"Jab aapko kisi tense moment mein stress ya overwhelm feel ho, toh slow breathing ek powerful tool ban sakti hai. Jab hum tense hote hain, hamari breathing shallow aur fast ho jati hai, jo body ke acute stress response ko trigger karti hai. Isko balance karne ke liye, aap ek simple technique try kar sakte hain: apne nose se deep breath lein, aur phir slow aur controlled tarike se exhale karein. Yeh practice parasympathetic nervous system ko activate karke heart rate ko calm karti hai. Sirf ek ya do minute ki conscious slow breathing aapko immediately ground karne mein help karti hai. Yeh educational awareness ke liye hai; agar stress persistent ho, toh professional support zaroor lein."
