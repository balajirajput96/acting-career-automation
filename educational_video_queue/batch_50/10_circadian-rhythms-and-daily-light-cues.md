## Narration Script (124 Words)

> "Hamari body ke andar ek natural 24-hour internal clock hoti hai, jise circadian rhythm kehte hain. Yeh cycle hamari sleep, alertness, aur body temperature ko regulate karti hai. Is clock ka sabse bada external cue light hai. Jab subah sunlight hamari eyes tak pahunchti hai, toh brain ka suprachiasmatic nucleus, yaani SCN, alert signals generate karta hai jo humein din bhar active rakhte hain. Iske opposite, jab shaam ko light kam hoti hai, toh brain melatonin hormone release karta hai jo relaxation aur sleep ko support karta hai. Lekin, evening me artificial screen light ya bright rooms me rehne se biological clock confuse ho sakti hai, jisse natural rest cycle disturb ho sakti hai. Isliye, daily natural light cues ko follow karna beneficial hai."
