## 1. Topic Identification
- **Topic Number:** 34
- **Track:** Neuroscience
- **Title:** The placebo effect: expectation is not a cure

## 2. Source Ledger
1. **Source Title:** The power of the placebo effect
   - **Organization:** Harvard Health Publishing
   - **URL:** https://www.health.harvard.edu/newsletter_article/the-power-of-the-placebo-effect
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Placebos may make individuals feel better by influencing subjective symptom perception, but they do not cure underlying medical conditions.

2. **Source Title:** Neurobiological Mechanisms of the Placebo Effect
   - **Organization:** National Institutes of Health (NIH) / PMC (Benedetti et al.)
   - **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6725834/
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** The placebo effect is a psychobiological phenomenon driven by expectation of clinical improvement and physiological pathways such as endogenous opioids.

3. **Source Title:** The neuroscience of placebo effects: connecting context, learning, and health
   - **Organization:** National Institutes of Health (NIH) / PMC (Wager & Atlas)
   - **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6013051/
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Beneficial placebo responses are attributable to brain-mind reactions to the treatment context and anticipation rather than active pharmacological agents.

## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
Educational content only. This video does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatments, promise clinical results, claim cures, or substitute for professional medical care. While expectation and neurobiological modulation can reduce subjective symptom perception (such as perceived pain), they do not alter underlying disease pathology or replace medical interventions.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (107 words)
Jab humein koi dawa milti hai, toh hamara dimaag expectation ke zariye physical response ko modulate karta hai—ise placebo effect kehte hain. Harvard Health ke anusar, placebos pain management aur symptom perception mein relief de sakte hain, lekin yeh underlying disease ko cure nahi karte. Neuroscience studies dikhati hain ki anticipation se prefrontal cortex aur endogenous opioid systems activate hote hain, jo subjective symptoms ko reduce karte hain. Lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki dawa ki zaroorat khatam ho gayi. Placebo effect expectation aur context par depend karta hai, cure par nahi. Yeh samajhna zaroori hai ki symptom modulation aur actual disease eradication mein farak hota hai.
