## Limitations and Safety Note

This educational review strictly adheres to non-clinical and non-diagnostic boundaries. The material presented is intended solely for general neuroscience education and conceptual understanding. It does not diagnose sleep disorders, prescribe medical interventions, promise cognitive enhancement, or substitute for professional medical care. 

Individuals experiencing chronic sleep disturbances, severe fatigue, or neurological symptoms are advised to consult qualified healthcare professionals. No fear-based messaging, sensational claims, or individualized lifestyle prescriptions are included in this package.

## Hindi-English Narration Script

The narration script is crafted for a neutral, calm, faceless educational delivery. It spans exactly 113 words, falling squarely within the required 105–125-word window.

> Jab aap raat ko sote hain, toh aapka dimag sirf rest nahi kar raha hota; woh memories ko actively organize kar raha hota hai. Neuroscience research kehti hai ki din bhar mein seekhi gayi cheezein temporary storage yaani hippocampus se long-term cortical networks mein transfer hoti hain. Is critical process ko **memory consolidation** kehte hain. Non-REM aur REM dono sleep stages isme alag-alag role play karte hain—kuch factual data ko stable banate hain, toh kuch physical skills ko refine karte hain. Jab sleep cycle complete hoti hai, toh neural connections mazboot hote hain, jisse learning aur retention behtar hoti hai. Isliye, adequate rest cognitive health aur optimal daily functioning ke liye essential hai.
