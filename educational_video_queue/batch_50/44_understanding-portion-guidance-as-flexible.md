## Hindi-English Narration Script
The following script is meticulously crafted for neutral faceless narration, containing **118 words** of balanced bilingual content that bridges formal public health principles with accessible conversational delivery.

> Portion guidance is often seen as a strict set of rules, but public health frameworks are designed to be flexible tools. जब हम portion sizes की बात करते हैं, तो rigid rules follow करने की जरूरत नहीं होती। Public health experts emphasize that visual guides, like plate proportions or hand measures, provide a general framework rather than exact measurements. Body hunger cues, daily activity levels, and individual energy needs vary widely from person to person. Flexibility means adjusting food amounts based on natural satiety and lifestyle, rather than counting every gram rigidly. By viewing portion guidance as a flexible reference instead of a rigid prescription, people can build sustainable, balanced eating habits over time. Consult a registered dietitian or healthcare provider for personalized nutrition guidance.
