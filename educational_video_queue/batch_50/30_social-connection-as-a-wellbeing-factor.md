## Limitations and Safety Framework
This educational video production adheres strictly to non-clinical public health communication standards. It is designed exclusively for health literacy enhancement and does not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or therapeutic treatment. 

> **Important Safety & Professional Disclaimer:** Content presented in this educational package avoids guarantees of specific health outcomes, absolute cures, and fear-based messaging. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care or clinical consultation. If individuals experience severe psychological distress, isolation, or crisis, they are advised to consult qualified healthcare professionals or established support helplines. ## Bilingual Narration Script (112 Words)
The script is written in neutral, balanced Hindi-English (Hinglish) suitable for faceless voiceover narration. The word count is strictly controlled at **112 words**, fitting comfortably within the 105–125 word limit required for a 60-second vertical video.

> Mental health literacy mein, social connection ko ek vital wellbeing factor maana gaya hai. Public health research ke anusar, strong social bonds mental resilience build karte hain aur daily stress ko reduce karne mein madad karte hain. CDC aur WHO ki guidelines batati hain ki meaningful relationships emotional support provide karte hain, jo overall daily functioning aur life satisfaction ko improve karta hai. Iska yeh arth hai ki regular positive interactions maintain karna hamare emotional health ke liye behad zaroori hai. Agar aapko excessive isolation ya continuous stress feel ho raha hai, toh kisi trusted professional, counselor, ya helpline se connect karna ek safe step hai. Apne social connections ko priority dijiye.
