## 3. Limitations and Safety Note

This educational module strictly adheres to clinical safety guidelines. It is intended solely for general scientific literacy and does not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment prescription, or individualized health counseling. Sleep disturbances can arise from complex physiological, psychological, or environmental pathologies that require professional clinical evaluation. The narrative strictly avoids alarmism, sensationalism, or absolute claims, acknowledging the evolving nature of empirical research on digital media habits.

## 4. Narration Script (Hindi-English / Bilingual)

> Neuroscience mein ek common sawaal hai: kya rat ko screen dekhne se neend kharab hoti hai? Harvard Health aur Sleep Foundation ke research kehte hain ki electronic devices ki blue light melatonin hormone ke production ko delay kar sakti hai, jo hamare circadian rhythm ko signal karta hai ki sleep ka time ho gaya hai. Isse sona thoda mushkil ho sakta hai. Lekin recent studies yeh bhi batati hain ki iska impact sab par ek jaisa nahi hota. Screen use aur sleep quality ke beech ka connection complex hai, jisme content type aur individual habits bhi role play karte hain. Toh jabki blue light biology ko affect karti hai, apne sleep patterns ko samajhna aur moderation rakhna sabse important hai.
