## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
- **Educational Scope Only:** This content is strictly educational and does not diagnose mental health conditions, prescribe treatments, promise cures, or substitute for professional medical, psychiatric, or psychological care.
- **Boundaries and Safety:** Emphasizes respecting personal boundaries and guiding individuals toward licensed professional support when distress is severe or persistent. Avoids fear-mongering, sensationalism, and individualized clinical claims.

## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script (115 Words)
> "Doston, checking in on a friend starts with care and active listening. Agar aapko lag raha hai ki aapka dost kisi mushkil daur se guzar raha hai, toh pehla kadam hai safe space banana. Start by asking open questions like, 'Hey, I noticed you have been quiet lately. Kaise ho aap?' Avoid judgment or quick advice. Instead, practice empathetic listening—yeh samajhna ki unhe kaisa lag raha hai, bina beech mein toke. Say something supportive like, 'I am here for you, and we can navigate this together.' Respect their boundaries; agar woh turant baat nahi karna chahte, toh unhe space dein. Remember, peer support does not replace professional care. If things feel heavy, encourage professional support."
