# INTERNAL REVIEW PACKAGE — TOPIC 20

## 1. Topic Identification and Overview
This internal review package addresses **Topic 20** within the **Diet literacy** track, titled **"Fruit and vegetables: variety over perfection."** The core educational objective is to communicate evidence-based public health guidelines regarding dietary diversity, emphasizing that sustainable, everyday consumption of varied fruits and vegetables outweighs the pursuit of dietary perfection or restrictive superfood trends.

## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script
The following narration script is written in a neutral, faceless educational tone blending accessible English and conversational Hindi terminology, totaling exactly 119 words to fit a 60-second 9:16 vertical video layout.

> "Hello doston. When it comes to daily nutrition, perfection is not the goal—variety is. Public health guidelines emphasize consuming a colorful mix of fruits and vegetables rather than stressing over expensive superfoods. Different colors and types provide unique vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber that support overall well-being. Fresh, frozen, canned, and dried options all count, making healthy eating flexible and accessible for everyone. Aapko exotic ingredients ki zarurat nahi hai; everyday staples like carrots, spinach, bananas, and seasonal local produce deliver complete nutritional value. Focus on small, consistent additions to your meals rather than drastic dietary changes. Consistency and variety together form the foundation of sustainable health habits. Eat smart and prioritize long-term wellness over daily perfection."
