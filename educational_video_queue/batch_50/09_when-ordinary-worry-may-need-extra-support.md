## 4. Hindi-English Script (Faceless Narration)
**Word Count:** 124 words

Life mein occasional worry ya chinta hona ek normal part hai. Hum sabhi kabhi na kabhi stress ya future ki uncertainities ko lekar pareshan hote hain. Lekin, fark tab padta hai jab yah chinta daily life, work, aur relationships ko interfere karne lage.

Jab ordinary worry control se bahar ho jaye, constant fatigue ya restlessness bane rahe, aur daily functioning par asar padne lage, toh yah sign hota hai ki extra support ki zarurat hai. Mental health literacy hamein yehi pehchanne me madad karti hai ki kab professional guidance leni chahiye.

Early support lene se recovery asan hoti hai aur coping skills behtar hoti hain. Agar aapko lag raha hai ki worry aapke control me nahi hai, toh kisi qualified professional se consult karein.
