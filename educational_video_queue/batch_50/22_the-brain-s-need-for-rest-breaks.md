## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script (117 Words)
> Jab aap ghanto tak continuous focus ke sath kaam karte hain, toh cognitive fatigue accumulate hoti hai. Neuroscience kehti hai ki hamara brain uninterrupted concentration ke liye design nahi hai. Jab aap break lete hain, toh brain ka Default Mode Network activate hota hai. Iska kaam information process karna aur memory consolidate karna hai. National Institutes of Health ki research kehti hai ki rest ke dauran brain newly learned skills ko fast speed par replay karta hai, jisse synaptic connections strong hote hain. Yeh neural replay learning efficiency ko improve karta hai. Isliye, chote rest breaks lena time waste nahi, balki cognitive health ke liye ek biological requirement hai. Apne limits ko samjhiye aur balanced breaks lijiye.
