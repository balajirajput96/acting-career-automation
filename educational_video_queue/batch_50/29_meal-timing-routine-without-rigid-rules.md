## Hindi-English Script (121 Words)
> "Let's talk about meal timing. Khane ka ek regular routine hona zaroori hai, lekin rigid rules banana zaroori nahi hai. Science kehti hai ki hamare body ki internal clock, yaani circadian rhythm, consistent eating patterns ko pasand karti hai. Jab hum lagbhag ek hi samay par khana khate hain, toh digestion aur energy levels behtar rehte hain.
> 
> Lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki 1 minute ki bhi der hone par panic kiya jaye. Life unpredictable ho sakti hai. Thoda flexibility rakhna, jaise 30 se 60 minutes ka buffer dena, metabolic stress ko kam karta hai. Main baat yeh hai ki rigid restriction ki jagah predictable rhythm apnayein. Listen to your hunger cues, stay consistent when possible, and avoid strict guilt-driven clock-watching."
