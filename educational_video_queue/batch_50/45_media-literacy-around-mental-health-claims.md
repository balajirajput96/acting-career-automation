## 2. Evidence Ledger

## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
- **Scope**: Educational and informational overview only.
- **Constraints**: Does not diagnose mental health conditions, prescribe treatments, promise medical cures, use fear or sensational language, make individualized diet advice, or substitute for professional medical and psychological evaluation.
- **Safety Cue**: Includes professional support signposting where acute psychological distress is encountered. Never contains instructions to publish, upload, contact, or promote externally.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (116 words)
> Aaj hum baat karenge mental health claims aur media literacy ke bare mein. 
> Social media par health tips aur self-diagnosis content roz dikhta hai. Lekin kya yeh claims trustworthy hain? 
> Pehla step hai source check karna. Kya information kisi official health organization ya research study par based hai? 
> Doosra step hai sensational headlines aur oversimplified cures se bachna. Real mental health support complex aur evidence-based hota hai. 
> Jab bhi aap online content dekhein, hamesha credentials verify karein aur expert guidance ko prioritize karein. 
> Mental health literacy ka matlab hai reliable information ko pehchanna aur misinformation se bachna. 
> Agar acute distress ho, toh professional healthcare provider ya crisis helpline se contact karein. 
> Stay informed and safe online.
