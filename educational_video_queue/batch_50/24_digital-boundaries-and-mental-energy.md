# Internal Educational-Video Review Package

## Topic Identification and Overview

The assigned educational video review package corresponds to **Topic Number 24**, operating within the **Mental-health literacy** track under the title **Digital boundaries and mental energy**. The objective of this package is to synthesize empirical insights regarding digital consumption and cognitive fatigue into a neutral, highly structured 60-second vertical educational resource.

---

## Source Ledger

The empirical foundation of this review package relies upon peer-reviewed research and authoritative public health guidelines. The following table summarizes the authoritative sources utilized, their respective organizations, direct URLs, and precise supported claims.

| Source Title | Organization | Direct URL | Precise Supported Claim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Doomscrolling dangers | Harvard Health Publishing | [Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/mind-and-mood/doomscrolling-dangers) | Creating intentional digital boundaries gives the brain and body a necessary opportunity to recalibrate from constant digital overload and chronic information consumption. |
| Associations Between Screen Time Use and Health | Centers for Disease Control and Prevention | [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/pcd/issues/2025/24_0537.htm) | High screen time and lack of digital boundaries can displace essential health behaviors, impact focus, and elevate emotional distress. |
| Social Media and Mental Health: Benefits, Risks, and Opportunities | National Institutes of Health | [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7785056/) | Unregulated online engagement and constant connectivity contribute to cognitive fatigue and diminished mental energy, underscoring the need for structured tech limits. |

## Limitations and Safety Note

> **Educational Scope Only:** This review package and its associated script are strictly intended for educational awareness regarding digital well-being and cognitive fatigue. Under no circumstances does this content diagnose clinical conditions, prescribe medical or psychological treatments, or serve as a substitute for professional mental health care.

For individuals experiencing persistent psychological distress, anxiety, or burnout impacting daily functioning, consulting a licensed mental health professional or qualified healthcare provider is strongly encouraged.

## Hindi-English Script (122 Words)

> "Aaj ki digital duniya mein, notifications aur endless scrolling hamari mental energy ko drain karte hain. Research batati hai ki excessive screen time hamare focus aur emotional well-being ko negatively impact karta hai. Iska solution kya hai? Healthy digital boundaries banana. Iska matlab hai ki hum apne online time ke liye clear limits set karein—jaise sleeping hours mein phone ko door rakhna ya notifications off karna. Jab hum intentionally digital breaks lete hain, toh hamare brain ko recalibrate karne ka mauka milta hai aur mental energy restore hoti hai. Small steps se aap apna digital space balance kar sakte hain. Agar digital stress aapki daily life ko affect kare, toh kisi mental health expert se support lena ek helpful step hai."
