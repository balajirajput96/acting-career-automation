## 4. Hindi-English Script (119 Words)
> "A balanced plate banana kitna zaroori hai? Let's break it down scientifically. 
> According to ICMR-NIN dietary guidelines and Harvard School of Public Health, 
> a healthy meal is about proper proportions and food variety. 
> Picture your plate divided simply: half your plate should be filled with colourful 
> vegetables and fresh fruits providing essential vitamins, minerals, and fibre. 
> One-quarter of the plate should contain whole grains like brown rice, millets, or whole wheat 
> chapatis for sustained energy. The remaining quarter is for quality protein sources 
> such as pulses, legumes, eggs, fish, or dairy, supporting daily vitality. 
> Remember, healthy eating is a gradual, sustainable habit, not a strict medical fix. 
> Always consult a qualified clinical dietitian or doctor for personalized dietary advice."
