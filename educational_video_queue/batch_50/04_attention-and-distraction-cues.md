## Narration Script (Hindi-English, 120 Words)

Hamara brain constant attention aur distraction cues ke beech balance maintain karta hai. Jab hum kisi task par focus karte hain, toh prefrontal cortex aur parietal network goal-directed attention control support karte hain. Lekin jab environment mein sudden visual ya auditory cues aate hain, toh involuntary attention switching trigger ho sakti hai. Yeh distraction cues hamari working memory aur cognitive control ko test karte hain. Neuroscience research batati hai ki brain ke selective filtering mechanisms, jaise distractor suppression, humein irrelevant stimuli ignore karne mein help karte hain. Is process ko samajhna cognitive efficiency improve karne ke liye useful hai. Focus ko sustain karne ke liye mindful pause lena aur task cues ko optimize karna ek effective strategy ho sakti hai.
