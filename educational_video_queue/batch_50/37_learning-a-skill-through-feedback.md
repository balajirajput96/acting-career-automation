## Limitations and Safety Note

This educational package is designed strictly for informational purposes to explain the neuroscientific mechanisms underlying motor skill acquisition and error-based feedback loops. It does not diagnose medical or neurological conditions, prescribe treatments or physical therapy regimens, promise rapid skill mastery, claim neurological cures, or substitute for professional clinical or neurological care. No fear-based or sensational language is employed; the tone remains strictly neutral, scientific, and objective.

## Hindi-English Script (109 Words)

Jab bhi hum koi naya skill seekhte hain, hamara brain ek continuous feedback loop me kaam karta hai. Neuroscience kehta hai ki jab movement ya action me error hota hai, toh cerebellum aur basal ganglia us error ko detect karte hain. Ye brain regions prediction error signals generate karte hain, jisse nervous system apne neural commands ko adjust karta hai. Is process ko error-based learning kehte hain. Jab aap baar-baar practice karte hain aur feedback ke basis par corrections apply karte hain, toh synaptic plasticity ke zariye neural pathways strong ho jaate hain. Is tarah, consistent feedback aur active error correction skill acquisition ko fast aur precise banate hain.
