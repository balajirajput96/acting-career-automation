## 3. Limitations and Safety Note

- **Individual Variance:** Fluid requirements vary based on age, sex, body weight, environmental climate, and physical activity. Individuals with specific medical conditions (such as renal, cardiac, or hepatic disorders) or those on fluid restrictions must consult qualified healthcare professionals for tailored guidance.
- **Tone Guardrails:** No fear-based messaging, exaggerated health claims, guaranteed outcomes, or individualized diagnostic prescriptions are permitted.

## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script (105–125 Words)
Staying hydrated is essential for your body's daily physiological functioning, but tracking your intake does not have to feel complicated. Rozmarra ki busy life mein proper hydration maintain karne ke liye kuch simple aur practical everyday cues follow kar sakte hain. Major public health organizations note that fluid needs vary by individual, but building steady routines prevents mild dehydration. Pehla practical cue: Subah din ki shuruat ek glass saaf paani se karein. Dusra cue: Apne workspace par paani ki bottle rakhein aur har meal ke sath regular sip karein. Teesra cue: Pyaas ka wait kiye bina din bhar hydration maintain karein, aur light yellow urine ek healthy indicator hai. Listen to your body and consult qualified professionals for personalized medical needs.
