## 4. Hindi-English Script (105–125 Words)

> Namaste dosto. Aaj hum baat karenge sodium aur high salt foods ko spot karne ke baare mein. World Health Organization guidelines kehti hain ki daily salt intake five grams yaani ek teaspoon se kam honi chahiye. Lekin hamari diet ka zyada-tar sodium table salt se nahi, balki packaged aur processed foods se aata hai jahan sodium preservative ka kaam karta hai. Nutrition labels check karte samay yaad rakhein: five percent Daily Value ya usse kam sodium low maana jata hai, jabki twenty percent ya zyada high hota hai. Common packaged items jaise sauces, canned soups, aur processed meats mein sodium high hota hai. Always check nutrition labels to make healthy choices.

*(Script Word Count: 111 words)*
