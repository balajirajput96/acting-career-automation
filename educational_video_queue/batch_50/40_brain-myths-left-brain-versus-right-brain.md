## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script (116 Words)
Kya aapne kabhi suna hai ki kuch log left brain ke hisaab se categorized hote hain? Yeh ek common neuromyth hai. Science kehti hai ki aisi koi whole brain dominance nahi hoti. 

Jab hum functional MRI scans analyze karte hain, toh researchers ko pata chalta hai ki dono hemispheres milkar complex tasks ko handle karte hain. 

Haalaanki kuch functions, jaise language, specific regions mein lateralized hote hain, lekin overall personality ke liye koi left ya right bias nahi milta. 

Hum apne poore dimag ka istemaal karte hain. Isliye, left brain aur right brain ki yeh theory sirf ek popular metaphor hai, scientific fact nahi. Hamesha evidence based neuroscience par bharosa karein aur misinformation se door rahein.
