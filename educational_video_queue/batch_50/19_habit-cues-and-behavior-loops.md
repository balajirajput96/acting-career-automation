## Hindi-English Narration Script (122 Words)

Have you ever wondered why your brain automatically reaches for your phone the moment you wake up? Neuroscience shows that habits operate through a continuous neural loop: **cue, routine, and reward**. 

Jab aapko koi external trigger ya **cue** milta hai—jaise phone ki notification—aapka brain ek automatic **routine** trigger karta hai. Yeh behavior **basal ganglia** aur specifically striatum mein store ho jata hai, jisse prefrontal cortex ko har baar decide nahi karna padta. 

Akhir mein, reward dopamine release karta hai, jo is loop ko mazeed strong banata hai. Iska matlab hai ki habit badalne ke liye, purane cue ko hatane ki jagah, ek naya routine insert karna zyada effective hota hai. Yeh neurological automation brain ki energy save karne ka natural tareeqa hai.
