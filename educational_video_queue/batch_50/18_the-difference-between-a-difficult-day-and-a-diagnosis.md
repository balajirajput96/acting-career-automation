# Internal Educational-Video Review Package: Topic 18

## Topic Identification and Metadata
The assigned educational video module focuses on mental-health literacy, specifically addressing **Topic 18: The difference between a difficult day and a diagnosis**. This internal review package establishes the evidentiary foundation, safety boundaries, bilingual script, visual storyboard, and caption plan required for production quality control and compliance verification.

## Evidence Ledger
The evidentiary foundation for this educational module is derived from authoritative public health and psychiatric institutions. The following structured ledger details the source organizations, direct URLs, and precise supported claims underpinning the script content.

| Source Title | Organization | URL | Precise Supported Claim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mental Health: What's Normal, What's Not | Mayo Clinic | https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/adult-health/in-depth/mental-health/art-20044098 | Temporary emotional distress or difficult days are common life occurrences, whereas mental health conditions involve ongoing symptoms that cause frequent distress and disrupt daily functioning, requiring formal clinical evaluation. |

| Caring for Your Mental Health | National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) | https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health | Understanding emotional well-being involves recognizing when routine stress transitions into persistent mental health concerns that benefit from professional guidance and support resources. |

## Limitations and Safety Framework
Strict educational boundaries govern this production. The content is designed exclusively for public mental health literacy and awareness. Under no circumstances does the module provide medical diagnoses, psychological evaluation, treatment prescriptions, individualized diet advice, or promises of cures. It is not a substitute for professional clinical care. 

For mental health content, an appropriate support and safety cue is integrated to direct viewers toward professional guidance: if a viewer or someone they know experiences prolonged or overwhelming emotional distress, they are encouraged to consult qualified healthcare professionals, counselors, or recognized crisis support helplines. No promotional instructions or external calls to action are included.

## Hindi-English Script (Faceless Narration)
The following bilingual script is crafted for neutral faceless narration, adhering strictly to the required word count of 105 to 125 words (measured at exactly 125 words).

> Har kisi ke jeevan mein difficult days aate hain—jab stress, low mood ya fatigue feel hoti hai. Yeh temporary emotional responses hote hain jo usual life stressors jaise work pressure ya personal challenges ke karan hote hain. Lekin ek mental health diagnosis tab consider kiya jata hai jab yeh feelings persistent hon, duration lambi ho, aur daily functioning, work, ya relationships ko severely impact karein. Clinical diagnosis ke liye specific medical criteria aur duration ki requirement hoti hai, jo transient emotional distress se alag hoti hai. Agar aapko lagta hai ki aap ya koi aur continuous struggle face kar raha hai, toh professional support ya counselor se consult karna helpful ho sakta hai. Awareness rakhna aur farak samajhna healthy well-being ki taraf pehla step hai.
