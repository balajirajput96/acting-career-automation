## Evidence Ledger
The research supporting this package is derived from authoritative governmental and academic public health institutions. The table below summarizes the source ledger, organizational affiliation, verified URL, and precise supported claims.

| Source Title | Organization | URL | Supported Claim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Limitations and Safety Note
This review package adheres strictly to non-diagnostic, educational-only standards. The content does not diagnose health conditions, prescribe treatments, promise specific health results, claim cures, use fear or sensational language, provide individualized dietary advice, or substitute for professional clinical care. Viewers seeking tailored dietary interventions or medical guidance must consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

## Hindi-English Narration Script (121 Words)
Welcome to diet literacy. Aaj hum baat karenge protein foods across common Indian meals. Protein hamare body ke tissues aur muscles ke repair aur growth ke liye zaroori nutrient hai. Indian thali mein dal, rajma, chana, paneer, dahi, aur whole grains ke combination se complete amino acid profile mil sakti hai. Jaise ki dal ke sath chawal ya roti khane se essential amino acids balance hote hain. National Institute of Nutrition ke anusaar, balanced diet mein plant-based pulses, dairy, aur lean sources ko daily meals mein shamil karna chahiye. Excessive protein supplements ki zaroorat nahi hoti jab aap apni traditional meals ko smartly plan karein. Yeh jankari keval educational purpose ke liye hai aur kisi medical advice ka vikalp nahi hai.

## Five-Scene 9:16 Faceless Visual Plan
The visual plan is structured into five distinct 9:16 vertical scenes designed for neutral faceless narration and clear typographic delivery:

1. **Scene 1 (0:00–0:12):** Features a minimalist split-screen graphic displaying the title "Diet Literacy: Protein in Indian Meals" over a clean background with subtle line-art representing a traditional Indian thali.
2. **Scene 2 (0:12–0:24):** Utilizes clean typographic overlays highlighting cellular repair and muscle function icons, paired with an animated graphic illustrating amino acid building blocks.
3. **Scene 3 (0:24–0:38):** Displays a top-down animated illustration of an Indian plate featuring dal, roti, rice, paneer, and curd, emphasizing how cereal-pulse combinations create complete protein profiles.
4. **Scene 4 (0:38–0:50):** Highlights text callouts quoting NIN guidelines on incorporating whole pulses, lentils, and dairy daily without relying on excessive commercial protein supplements.
5. **Scene 5 (0:50–1:00):** Presents a clean summary screen displaying the educational disclaimer and a reminder to consult qualified nutrition professionals.
