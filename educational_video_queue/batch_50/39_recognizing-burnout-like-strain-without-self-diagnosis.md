## Limitations and Safety Compliance
The review package strictly complies with foundational public health safety boundaries. The content is strictly educational in nature, designed exclusively to enhance mental-health literacy without engaging in clinical diagnosis or therapeutic advice. 

> **Safety & Scope Boundary**: This educational material does not diagnose medical or psychological conditions, prescribe treatments, promise specific outcomes, claim cures, or employ fear-based or sensational language. Furthermore, it avoids individualized dietary or lifestyle prescriptions and emphasizes that educational awareness cannot substitute for formal professional care. Viewers experiencing persistent distress are guided toward qualified healthcare professionals.

## Narration Script (Hindi-English, 114 Words)
The script is crafted for neutral, faceless educational narration in an accessible Hindi-English blend, strictly maintaining a supportive, non-sensational tone across exactly 114 words.

> Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki aap poori tarah drained hain? Constant work pressure ya academic load ki wajah se energy depletion feel hona normal lag sakta hai, lekin kya yeh burnout hai ya sirf temporary exhaustion? 
> Mental-health literacy ke mutabiq, burnout-like strain ko pehchanna zaroori hai, par khud ko diagnose karna sahi nahi hai. WHO ke anusaar, burnout ek occupational phenomenon hai, koi medical disease nahi. Isme chronic stress, mental detachment, aur reduced efficacy shamil hoti hai. 
> Agar aapko aisi feeling lagataar reh rahi hai, toh jaldi se kisi self-diagnosis par mat puchiye. Ek professional expert se consult karna aur apni recovery par dhyan dena hi sabse behtar step hai. Stay aware, take care!
