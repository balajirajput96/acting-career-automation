## 4. Narration Script (Hindi-English)
> **Word Count Check:** 121 words (Target: 105–125 words)

"Navigating nutrition labels for added sugars does not have to be stressful. Food packaging often lists total sugars, but checking the ingredient list and the 'Added Sugars' line helps us understand what is naturally occurring versus what has been added during processing. Regulatory bodies like the US FDA and India's ICMR-NIN emphasize that while natural sugars in whole fruits and milk are part of a balanced diet, limiting added sugars supports long-term metabolic health. Chini ya added sugars se darne ki zaroorat nahi hai; awareness hi asli key hai. Jab hum packaged items par 'Added Sugars' line check karte hain, toh hum informed choices bana sakte hain bina kisi guilt ya fear ke. Small, mindful label reads build sustainable lifelong habits."
