## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
This educational review package is strictly designed for informational literacy and awareness. It does not provide medical diagnoses, prescribe treatments, promise specific outcomes, claim cures, use fear-based or sensational language, offer individualized diet advice, or substitute for professional clinical care. Appropriate support and urgent-safety cues are integrated solely for educational clarity regarding crisis intervention. No instructions to publish, upload, contact, or promote externally are included.

## 4. Hindi-English Narration Script
**Duration:** 60 Seconds  
**Word Count:** 123 Words  

Mental health literacy mein ye samajhna bahut zaroori hai ki kab immediate safety ke liye urgent help seek karni chahiye. Kuch warning signs par dhyan dena hamari suraksha ke liye critical hota hai. Agar koi vyakti suicide ya self-harm ki baat kare, extreme hopelessness feel kare, ya apna ya dusron ka nuksan karne ki plan banaye, toh yeh ek immediate crisis ka sign hai. Iske alawa, sudden dramatic behavioral changes, severe confusion, hallucinations, ya daily functioning ka poori tarah collapse hona bhi urgent professional care ki demand karta hai. Aise samay mein wait nahi karna chahiye. Agar aap ya aapka koi janne wala crisis mein hai, toh turant helpline par contact karein. Yeh educational guidance hai, medical diagnosis nahi. Apni safety priority dein.
