## 2. Source Ledger
The evidentiary foundation for this review package is derived from three authoritative public health and food safety institutions. Table 1 summarizes the source titles, governing organizations, URLs, and precise supported claims.

| Source Title | Organization | URL | Precise Supported Claim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Preventing Food Poisoning: Food Safety | Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) | [CDC Food Safety Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/prevention/index.html) | Perishable foods must not be left at room temperature for more than two hours to prevent rapid bacterial proliferation and foodborne illness. |
| Leftovers and Food Safety | U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) FSIS | [USDA Leftovers Guidance](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety) | Cooked leftovers should be stored in shallow containers for rapid cooling and consumed within three to four days when maintained in refrigerator storage. |
| 4 Steps to Food Safety | FoodSafety.gov (HHS) | [FoodSafety.gov 4 Steps](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety) | Leftovers must be reheated thoroughly to a safe internal temperature (74°C / 165°F) before consumption to destroy potential pathogens. |

## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
This educational content is strictly designed for general public hygiene awareness and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition or foodborne illness. Viewers are advised that if perishable food has been kept in the temperature danger zone for over two hours, or if stored leftovers exhibit unusual odor, texture, or discoloration, they must be discarded immediately. Visual inspection and smell alone are never reliable indicators of food safety.

## 4. Narration Script (Hindi-English)
The script below is written for neutral faceless narration, blending English and Hindi naturally. The word count is exactly 110 words, falling within the mandated 105–125 word range.

> Welcome to today's food safety guide. Leftovers are convenient, par safe handling is essential. Sabse pehle, yaad rakhiye: cooked food ko do ghante ke andar refrigerator mein rakhna chahiye. Room temperature par chhorne se bacteria tezi se badhte hain. Second, use shallow containers taaki food jaldi thanda ho sake. Jab aap leftovers consume karein, toh unhe proper temperature tak reheat karna zaroori hai. Generally, 74 degrees Celsius tak garam karein taaki pathogens destroy ho jayein. Lastly, most cooked leftovers ko refrigerator mein teen se char din ke andar finish kar lena chahiye. Jab doubt ho, toh throw it out. Stay safe, eat fresh, and follow proper food hygiene every day.
