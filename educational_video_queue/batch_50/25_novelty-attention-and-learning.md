It does not diagnose, treat, or offer medical advice for neurological or psychological conditions. The content avoids sensationalized claims, exaggerated "brain hacks," or fear-based messaging regarding routine learning challenges. Individuals experiencing persistent cognitive difficulties or clinical attention deficits should consult qualified healthcare professionals.
