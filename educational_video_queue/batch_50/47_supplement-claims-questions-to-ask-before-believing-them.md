## 2. Source Ledger

   - **Organization:** U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
   - **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/food/information-consumers-using-dietary-supplements/questions-and-answers-dietary-supplements
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** The FDA does not pre-approve dietary supplement claims for efficacy against diseases, and manufacturers are responsible for ensuring product safety and truthful labeling.

3. **Title:** Dietary Supplements: The Nutrition Source
   - **Organization:** Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health
   - **URL:** https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/dietary-supplements/
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Health claims on supplements that imply treatment or cure for medical conditions should be questioned, as marketing often relies on unscientific anecdotes rather than independent research.

## 3. Limitations and Safety Note
- **Educational Scope Only:** This video provides general educational literacy on evaluating supplement marketing claims and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment prescription.
- **Individual Consultation:** Viewers must consult qualified healthcare professionals before taking any new dietary supplement, especially if managing existing health conditions or taking prescription medications.
- **Safety Warning:** Avoid products making unverified miracle cure or rapid disease-reversal claims.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (Word Count: 110)
Market mein naye supplement claims dekh kar kya aap confuse hote hain? Before believing hype, yahan teen essential questions hain jo aapko zaroor puchne chahiye.

First: Kya yeh product kisi specific disease ko 'cure' ya 'treat' karne ka claim karta hai? Remember, dietary supplements are regulated differently from medicines, and structural health claims must be backed by evidence, not unscientific anecdotes.

Second: Is the scientific evidence independent and peer-reviewed, ya sirf manufacturer testimonials par depend karta hai? Always check reliable public health sources like NIH or FDA.

Third: Have you consulted a qualified healthcare professional before starting? Supplements can interact with medications. Stay informed, stay safe, and evaluate facts critically.
