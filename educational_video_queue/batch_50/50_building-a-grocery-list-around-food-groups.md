## Hindi-English Script (121 Words)
Building a smart grocery list around core food groups is the foundation of daily diet literacy. Jab aap grocery store jaate hain, to apne basket ko balanced food groups mein divide karein: vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean proteins, aur dairy ya plant-based alternatives. According to public health authorities like USDA MyPlate and ICMR-NIN, organizing purchases by food groups ensures a diverse intake of essential nutrients and micronutrients. Start by selecting fresh or frozen colorful vegetables and whole fruits. Next, add whole grains like brown rice or millets, and lean protein sources such as lentils, beans, fish, or poultry. Plan ahead to avoid impulsive purchases and reduce food waste. Consult a qualified dietitian for personalized nutritional needs. Stay informed and eat mindfully.
