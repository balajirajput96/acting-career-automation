## 2. Source Ledger
1. **Source Title:** The sleep-deprived human brain
   - **Organization:** National Institutes of Health (PMC)
   - **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6143346/
   - **Supported Claim:** Sleep loss decreases task-related activation in fronto-parietal attention networks and diminishes functional connectivity within the brain.

2. **Source Title:** How Acute Total Sleep Loss Affects the Attending Brain
   - **Organization:** National Institutes of Health (PMC)
   - **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4288604/
   - **Supported Claim:** Acute sleep deprivation directly reduces brain activation in key regions such as the prefrontal cortex and intraparietal sulcus, impairing sustained attention.

3. **Source Title:** Sharpen thinking skills with a better night's sleep
   - **Organization:** Harvard Health Publishing
   - **URL:** https://www.health.harvard.edu/mind-and-mood/sharpen-thinking-skills-with-a-better-nights-sleep
   - **Supported Claim:** Inadequate sleep leads to declined attention, slower reaction times, and increased inattentiveness during cognitive tasks.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (114 Words)
Jab hum poori neend nahi lete, toh hamare dimag ke fronto-parietal attention network par seedha asar padta hai. Prefrontal cortex, jo ki focus aur executive control ke liye zaroori hai, ki activity kam ho jati hai. Yeh wahi hissa hai jo humein distraction se bachata hai aur task par dhyan lagane mein madad karta hai. Neend ki kami se neurons ki firing slow ho jati hai, jisse lapses of attention aur slow reaction times badh jaate hain. Iska matlab yeh hai ki jab rest poora nahi hota, toh brain ko information process karne mein zyada mehnat karni padti hai. Professional ya daily tasks mein focus banaye rakhne ke liye quality sleep behad zaroori hai.
