## 2. Evidence Ledger
1. **Source Title:** Effect of Inadequate Sleep on Frequent Mental Distress
   - **Organization:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
   - **URL:** https://www.cdc.gov/pcd/issues/2021/20_0573.htm
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Individuals experiencing inadequate sleep (averaging 6 hours or less per night) demonstrate significantly higher likelihoods of frequent mental distress and emotional strain.
2. **Source Title:** Sleep and Mental Health
   - **Organization:** Harvard Health Publishing
   - **URL:** https://www.health.harvard.edu/mindscape/for-parents-and-caregivers/mental-wellness-basics/sleep
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Regular poor sleep sets the stage for negative thinking and emotional vulnerability, whereas consistent restorative sleep fosters mental and emotional balance.
3. **Source Title:** Optimizing Behavioral Sleep Interventions for Adolescents and Young Adults
   - **Organization:** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
   - **URL:** https://www.nimh.nih.gov/funding/grant-writing-and-application-process/concept-clearances/2023/optimizing-behavioral-sleep-interventions-for-adolescents-and-young-adults
   - **Precise Supported Claim:** Behavioral sleep patterns and consistency directly influence anxiety, mood regulation, and psychological resilience across developmental stages.

## 4. Hindi-English Script (124 Words)
Consistent sleep routines play a vital role in emotional wellbeing. 
(NARRATION: 'Roz ki neend aur sleep routine ka emotional health se connection hai.') 
Jab hum roz ek hi samay par sote aur jagte hain, toh nervous system stress hormones ko manage karta hai. 
(NARRATION: 'Consistent rest supports emotional stability.') 
Research indicates that chronic sleep disruption increases vulnerability to anxiety and emotional fatigue. 
(NARRATION: 'Kam neend se mental distress badh sakti hai.') 
Building a calming wind-down habit—such as minimizing screens before bed—signals the body that it is time to rest. 
(NARRATION: 'Sone se pehle screen time kam karna sleep quality improve karta hai.') 
Prioritizing consistent rest supports emotional resilience and daily functioning. 
(NARRATION: 'Behtar neend apnakar hum apni mental wellbeing ko sustain kar sakte hain.')
