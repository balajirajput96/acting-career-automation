# Daily Educational Video Package — Sleep and Memory

**Status:** `READY FOR INTERNAL ASSET REVIEW — NOT PUBLISHED`

## Educational Purpose

Explain in plain language that sleep supports memory formation and retention after learning. This is general education, not medical advice or a treatment claim.

## Source Ledger

| Source | Claim used | Limitation carried into script |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Harvard Medical School — Sleep and Memory](https://sleep.hms.harvard.edu/education-training/public-education/sleep-and-health-education-program/sleep-health-education-88) | Learning includes acquisition, consolidation, and recall; inadequate sleep appears to affect consolidation of factual and procedural memory. | The mechanism is still being studied; individual results vary. |
| [NIH News in Health — Sleep On It](https://newsinhealth.nih.gov/2013/04/sleep-it) | Sleep before and after learning supports memory formation and stabilization; all-nighters can impair learning. | This does not promise that sleep alone fixes learning or memory difficulty. |
| [Yale School of Medicine — Sleep’s Crucial Role in Preserving Memory](https://medicine.yale.edu/news-article/sleeps-crucial-role-in-preserving-memory/) | Research describes encoding and consolidation, with sleep—especially slow-wave sleep—thought to support longer-term memory storage. | The article describes ongoing research; it does not establish a personal diagnosis or treatment. |

## Narration Script

> **Hook:** Study karke raat bhar jagna smart shortcut lag sakta hai—but brain new information ko ek hi moment mein permanent save nahi karta.
>
> Learning ke teen parts samjho: acquire karna, consolidate karna, aur recall karna. Harvard aur NIH ki educational summaries ke mutabik, sleep ke baad new factual aur skill-based memories zyada stable banne mein support mil sakta hai.
>
> Iska matlab yeh nahi ki ek “perfect” night har test fix kar degi. But all-nighter ko strategy samajhna bhi strong plan nahi hai.
>
> Aaj ka practical step: padhai ke baad teen key points likho, phir apne normal sleep routine ko protect karo. Agar sleep ya memory issues lagatar hain, clinician se baat karo.
>
> Source note: NIH, Harvard Sleep Medicine, and Yale School of Medicine.
